How I
think.

I don't separate brand from growth. I look at the whole system — what the business needs, what the audience needs, what the channel can deliver and what can actually be measured.

Brand is a business asset.

Positioning, identity and communication should make the commercial proposition easier to understand, remember and choose.

Systems beat heroics.

If a workflow depends on one person doing everything manually, it is not a scalable marketing system. Build the system.

Measure what matters.

Reach is useful. So are leads, conversion, ROAS, revenue and profit. The metric depends on the job the marketing is supposed to do.
